Games 3D Worlds & Scenes Web Pages & Tools Animation & Film Video-only demos Apps from Luvia

This page gathers playable works and open-source projects that people built with Claude Fable 5.2, drawn from the public list awesome-fable-5.2. Click a cover or Play to jump out and try the original; Source appears when the list recorded a repository.

All works belong to their creators. This is a curated guide to awesome-fable-5.2, not an official FABLE store and not affiliated with Anthropic. The list itself is CC0 1.0. Links in the list were last checked there on 27 November 2026; play and source links were checked again here on 28 November 2026.

You can also browse each work on the list's website, fablelist.000ooo.ooo.
